DM Meetings

Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 2026

DM Meetings is a personal application developed by Domenko Artem. This page explains what data the application accesses and how it is handled.

1. What data the application accesses

When you sign in with your Google account, DM Meetings requests access to:

  • Basic Google profile information (your email address and name) — used solely to identify you within the application.
  • Google Drive files — read and write access, used only to create, read, and modify files that you explicitly choose to work with through the application.

2. How your data is used

All data accessed through Google APIs is used exclusively to provide the functionality of DM Meetings. The application does not analyse, profile, or use your data for advertising or any other purpose.

3. Data sharing

Your data is not shared, sold, or transferred to any third party. No data obtained through Google APIs is transmitted to external servers operated by the developer.

4. Data storage

DM Meetings does not store your Google data on any server. Authentication tokens are kept locally on your own device and are used only to communicate directly with Google's APIs. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

DM Meetings' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Children's privacy

This application is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to this policy

Any updates to this policy will be published on this page with a revised date.

8. Contact
