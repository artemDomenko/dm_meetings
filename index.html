DM Meetings

App Information and Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 2026

About DM Meetings

DM Meetings is a personal application developed by Domenko Artem. Its purpose is to help the user manage meeting-related files and documents stored in their own Google Drive account. The application allows the user to read, create, and modify files in Google Drive in order to support meeting organisation and document workflow.

Purpose of the application: DM Meetings is a personal productivity tool that connects to Google Drive to help a single user organise and manage their own meeting documents. It is not a commercial product and is not offered as a public service.

How DM Meetings uses Google APIs

DM Meetings requests access to the following Google APIs when you sign in:

  • Google Sign-In (OpenID Connect) — to obtain your email address and basic profile information, so the application can identify you as the account owner.
  • Google Drive API (read and write) — to read, create, and modify files in your own Google Drive that you explicitly choose to work with through the application.

These permissions are used only for the functionality described above. The application does not access any other Google services and does not request permissions beyond what is necessary for its stated purpose.

Privacy Policy

1. What data DM Meetings accesses

When you sign in with your Google account, DM Meetings accesses the following categories of data:

  • Account identification data: your email address and display name, provided by Google Sign-In. This is used solely to identify you within the application.
  • Google Drive file data: files and folders in your Google Drive that the application reads, creates, or modifies at your explicit request. DM Meetings does not scan, analyse, or index your Drive content for any other purpose.

2. How your data is used

All data accessed through Google APIs is used exclusively to provide the core functionality of DM Meetings — namely, managing your meeting-related documents in Google Drive. Your data is never used for advertising, profiling, machine learning training, or any purpose unrelated to the application's stated function.

3. Data sharing and third parties

Your data is not shared, sold, rented, or transferred to any third party. DM Meetings does not operate any backend server that receives or stores your Google data. All communication with Google APIs happens directly between your device and Google's servers.

4. Data storage and retention

DM Meetings does not store your Google account data or Drive content on any external server. Authentication tokens issued by Google are stored locally on your own device and are used only to communicate with Google's APIs on your behalf.

You can revoke DM Meetings' access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Once access is revoked, the locally stored tokens become invalid and can no longer be used.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

DM Meetings' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the application's core functionality.
  • Data is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide that functionality or as required by law.
  • Data is not used for serving advertisements.
  • Data is not used to train machine learning models.
  • Human access to your data is not permitted, except with your explicit consent or as required by law.

6. Security

Because DM Meetings runs locally on your device and does not transmit your data to any external server controlled by the developer, the security of your data depends primarily on your device and your Google account security. We recommend using two-factor authentication on your Google account.

7. Children's privacy

DM Meetings is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect or process data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

Any updates to this privacy policy will be published on this page together with a revised "last updated" date. Continued use of the application after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
